## How does Y change the list?

For this list, “without vowels” means the written word contains none of A, E, I, O or U. Y is allowed by default. That convention includes CRYPT and MYRRH even though both words have a vowel sound when spoken. This is a spelling filter, so you can apply it from the colors on your board without making a decision about pronunciation.

### When Y is still available

Compare NYMPH and GLYPH. Both are 5 letter words without vowels, both end in PH, and both use Y. In NYMPH the Y is second. In GLYPH it is third. If a green square already fixes Y in third place, that single observation separates two words that otherwise look remarkably similar.

A yellow Y is a different clue. It tells you the letter belongs somewhere else. Leave its position open until the board confirms a green. If you also want to encode several yellow clues, the full Wordle Solver is a better place to keep them together. You can still browse 5 letter words without vowels to find words you recognize.

### When Y is also out

## Use the shape of a word to test a clue

Some 5 letter words without vowels are easier to see as smaller pieces. With GLYPH, the final PH is a helpful unit. With TRYST, the ending ST is familiar even if the start of the word looks odd. Keeping one piece still while you vary the other is a useful way to read a short candidate list.

### Look for a second copy of a letter

DRYLY, SHYLY, SLYLY and WRYLY all have Y in positions three and five. A single green Y in the middle leaves each one possible. Take a moment to try a second Y in the last square, especially if you have been mentally using every letter just once.

MYRRH offers a different reminder about 5 letter words without vowels. Its repeated letter is R, while Y appears once. If you start from a vague impression that every unusual word must repeat Y, the double R is easy to miss. Count the colored copies on your board before making an exclusion.

#### A three-word practice round

Take the last three 5 letter words without vowels from the worked board: GLYPH, SHYLY and SLYLY. Cover the list, choose one as a secret, and have a friend guess SHYLY. ## Questions that come up during a game

### Are there 5 letter words without vowels that use no Y?

Yes. CRWTH and CWTCH are two examples in the site's guess list. Use the Exclude Y control to inspect all six entries. If you are playing a different word game, check its own dictionary before spending a turn on a rare spelling. Different games can make different choices about acceptable guesses.

### Should I switch to this list after one gray opening word?

Check which vowels the opener actually tested. A gray A and E tell you nothing about I, O or U. An opener such as STARE leaves three conventional vowels untested. You can browse 5 letter words without vowels to get ideas at that point, but keep the ordinary vowel patterns in play until the feedback eliminates them.

#### What if I remember the ending but nothing else?

Open the full list of 5 letter words without vowels and pin the ending in its actual squares. For a remembered PH ending, put P fourth and H fifth. Compare the results with the smaller common reference view. GLYPH and NYMPH illustrate why pinning both squares is more informative than merely requiring a P somewhere in the word.

If you remember a word ending in LY, try that pair instead. SHYLY and SLYLY now sit side by side. Read the words aloud or think of the sentence you had in mind. One describes doing something bashfully, the other doing it craftily. That distinction can jog a memory that spelling alone did not recover.

#### Can I use the list for a classroom word challenge?

Ask two players to find 5 letter words without vowels with different endings. One might pick TRYST and another GLYPH. Then ask them to point to the letter doing the work of a vowel in each word. Keeping the written rule on screen gives everyone the same starting point while leaving plenty to discuss about sound.

### Why do sites list different numbers of 5 letter words without vowels?

A list built from past answers, a curated set of familiar words and a broad dictionary each starts with a different collection. Even two sites using large dictionaries can be on different versions. When comparing lists of 5 letter words without vowels, first check how each defines a vowel, then check which words qualified for inclusion.
