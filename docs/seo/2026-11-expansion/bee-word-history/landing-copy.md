## What counts as an eligible hive?

A word must be at least four letters long. It must use the required center letter, and every different letter in the word must appear among the hive's seven letters. A letter can be used more than once. These rules let us tell whether the board offered a word's letters independently of the words listed in the answer key.

## Was the word ever a pangram?

A pangram uses all seven different hive letters in one word. Its total length may be greater than seven because repeats are allowed. A word appearing on many dates can still have zero pangrams. ONION is an easy case to verify: its three distinct letters can never cover a seven-letter hive.

## Questions about looking up a word

### Can Spelling Bee word history tell me whether today's game accepts my word?

The historical lookup uses completed puzzles. A result such as 66 out of 66 describes that collection of past hives. For a question about a current board, use the game's own input or the dedicated hints for today's hive. This separation also lets you look up an old favorite without revealing today's answer list.

### Why does my spelling return no dates?

Check the exact word first. ONION and ONIONS have different letter sets, and adding S changes the hive requirements. Spelling Bee word history treats them as separate searches. Then read the opportunity count. Zero opportunities and nineteen opportunities with zero listings both yield an empty list of appearances, but the evidence behind the empty list differs.

#### Can I search for a short word or a phrase?

Use one English word containing at least four letters. A three-letter entry falls outside the game's length rule, and a string of more than seven different letters cannot fit one hive. Correcting an accidental space is usually a better first step than trying several related words and wondering why each gave a different answer.

#### How can I share a result accurately?

Include the word, the fraction and the coverage date. For example, “HIED was listed in seven of seventeen eligible hives through November 3, 2026” gives another player everything needed to check the claim. If you are discussing one disputed puzzle, add its date and center letter so the other player can open the same board.

### Why do two sources of Spelling Bee word history give different counts?

Compare the start date, the last completed hive and the spelling. Then check whether both archives are counting all eligible hives or only dates when the word appeared. A collection spanning more years can reasonably give a larger count. Comparing the overlapping dates makes two sets of Spelling Bee word history much easier to reconcile.
