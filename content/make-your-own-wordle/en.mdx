## How do you make your own Wordle for a friend?

Choose a five-letter secret, add a clue if you like, and select **Create challenge link**. Copy that link into the message you want to send. Your friend opens a blank board with six guesses. If you want to see how the invitation will feel first, select **Test as a player** and play a round in the preview.

The nicest reason to make your own Wordle is that you know something about the person solving it. A word that looks ordinary on a daily board can mean something quite specific to two friends. Think of a place you visited, a running joke, or the one animal a child kept going back to see at the zoo.

### Try an example before writing your first puzzle

Select **Load example** to put OTTER in the secret field. The accompanying clue mentions an animal floating on its back. Open the clue in the player preview, then try a guess. Notice which parts of the page belong to the sender. Your friend receives the board and the optional clue. The editing fields stay on the maker page.

| Who is playing? | A possible secret | A clue you could write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A friend who went camping with you | EMBER | What we watched after the last log burned down. |
| A child who likes animals | OTTER | You might spot one floating on its back. |
| Someone learning kitchen vocabulary | WHISK | The drawer utensil I reach for to beat an egg. |
| A family member who loves music | VIOLA | The string instrument between a violin and a cello. |

#### Start with the answer your friend would spell

Say the secret aloud and imagine your friend writing it down. Would you both choose the same spelling? A word with two accepted regional spellings can still make a fine challenge. Give the recipient a reason to choose the version you had in mind. When you make your own Wordle, that small courtesy can save an otherwise good final guess.

## Which words work when you make your own Wordle?

The secret can be any five letters from A to Z. A name or a family nickname works if it fits five tiles. Spaces, hyphens, numbers and accented characters do not fit this board. Capitalization makes no difference. If you enter the word in lowercase, the board will score guesses against the same five letters in uppercase.

### Can you make your own Wordle with a name?

Yes. The chosen answer is always a legal winning guess, including a spelling outside the regular guess list. Other guesses must belong to WordleHint’s five-letter word bank. This gives your friend room to start with everyday words and still finish with the particular nickname you chose.

If you make your own Wordle around a name, tell the player the category in the optional clue. “Someone who always volunteers to drive” leaves an interesting family mystery. Without that clue, a player might spend all six rows testing ordinary nouns because it never occurred to them that a person’s name could win.

#### How hard is a word with two copies of a letter?

Repeated letters give a player one more thing to discover. In the site’s 2,315-word common-answer reference list, 1,566 spellings use five different letters. The other 749 repeat at least one. The table counts distinct letters in each whole word. A spelling with two different doubled letters appears once in the three-distinct-letter row.

| Different letters in the word | Reference words | What the setter can expect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Five | 1,566 | Every green or yellow tile identifies a different letter. |
| Four | 691 | One letter occurs twice. |
| Three | 57 | A player may encounter two pairs or one letter used three times. |
| Two | 1 | The whole spelling is built from just two letters. |

These counts describe that reference list. They give you a sense of how unusual a pattern is when you make your own Wordle. They say little about how quickly your particular friend will recognize a word. A repeated-letter nickname you both use every day might be easier than a familiar-looking five-letter plant name.

## How do you write a clue when you make your own Wordle?

A clue should narrow the search while leaving the player a decision. Imagine setting EMBER. “Something hot” includes a stove, a drink and a summer afternoon. “What kept glowing after we put away the marshmallows” gives the player a picture and a manageable set of possibilities. Try reading it aloud without looking at the secret field.

### Give the player control over the hint

The clue starts folded on the recipient’s board. Players who enjoy working from tile colors can leave it closed. A beginner can read it before their first guess. You can make your own Wordle for two people of different experience levels and send the same invitation to both. Neither person’s choice changes the other person’s board.

#### Check what your clue gives away

The maker flags a clue that spells the whole secret as a separate word. It cannot judge every pun or personal reference. A clue that says “rhymes with a potter” may give OTTER away immediately to an adult, yet be exactly right for a young reader. Use the preview to read the clue from the player’s side before creating the link.

## What happens in the test board when you make your own Wordle?

Test play uses the same guess list, six-row limit and color rules as the recipient’s game. The test is separate from the invitation. ### Why does only one L in ALLEY turn yellow?

APPLE contains a single L in position four. In ALLEY, the L in position two claims the one available copy as a yellow tile. The L in position three stays gray. The gray tile tells you there is no second copy available for that guess. It does not cancel the yellow L sitting next to it.

On the next row, AMPLE fixes A, P, L and E in their exact places. The M is gray. APPLE then supplies the missing second-position P and completes the board. If you make your own Wordle for someone learning these rules, ask them to predict the colors of the first row before submitting it. The symbols under the letters let you discuss each tile by position.

#### Does a rejected guess use an attempt?

Only an accepted guess fills a row. An entry with the wrong length, or a non-answer spelling outside the guess list, leaves the counter where it was. Correct the entry and try again. Six accepted guesses without the secret end the round, at which point the player sees the word the setter chose.

## How do you send a challenge that opens on the right board?

After you make your own Wordle, use **Copy link** beneath the editor. The invitation points directly to the player page and includes the challenge after the hash sign. Copy the entire address. If you copy only the part before the hash, your friend will open the player page without a puzzle to load.

| What you want to send | What to copy | What the recipient gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A new puzzle for someone to solve | Challenge link from the maker | A playable blank board with the secret already set. |
| Your result after playing | Copy result on the finished board | The turn count and colored rows, with the letters left out. |
| An invitation for someone to set a word | The maker page address | A fresh editor where they can choose their own secret. |

### What can your friend see when you make your own Wordle?

The address carries an encoded copy of the puzzle. The secret and clue do not appear as ordinary words alongside the link, and the game keeps the answer off the board until the round ends. Someone who deliberately decodes the invitation can read its contents. Choose a playful secret that you are comfortable giving to anyone who receives the link.

When you make your own Wordle for a group chat, send a short invitation such as “I picked a word from our last trip.” Keep the answer out of the message and any accompanying screenshot. A screenshot of the maker can show the secret field even when the player preview beside it still shows five question marks.

#### Can you edit an invitation after copying it?

Each generated link carries the version you created at that moment. If you change the clue or the word, create and send a new link. The first one still leads to the original puzzle. This is useful when you make your own Wordle for two groups and want to give one group a more specific hint without changing the other group’s game.

## What happens if a player refreshes or wants a rematch?

Accepted rows are saved in the player’s current browser tab when session storage is available. Refresh the same challenge there and the game rebuilds the board from those rows. Text still sitting in the guess field has not been submitted. Someone opening the invitation on a different device starts with a fresh board.

### Which result can you share after you make your own Wordle?

A winning result begins with Custom Wordle and the number of guesses out of six. An unfinished search that used all six accepted rows is scored X/6. The copy contains one line of colored squares for each guess. You can compare where the first greens appeared before everyone in the chat has finished.

If you make your own Wordle as a return challenge, choose a new secret from the finish screen’s **Make a return puzzle** link. Your friend becomes the solver this time. Each invitation has its own board, so you can come back to the original conversation and compare both rounds.

#### Where can you practice before you make your own Wordle again?

Play a few random boards in [Wordle Unlimited](/wordle-unlimited) to see which clues you wish you had received. If a completed game left you wondering exactly when a guess ruled out an alternative, enter the finished sequence in the [Wordle Analyzer](/wordle-analyzer). Both exercises give you material for the next time you make your own Wordle for a friend.
