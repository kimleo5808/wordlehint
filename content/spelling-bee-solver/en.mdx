## How do I use the Spelling Bee Solver without seeing the answers?

Start with the smallest hint that will change how you play. If you have found one pangram and wonder whether another is hiding, the pangram count alone might be enough. If a five-letter word keeps slipping away, open the five-letter group and ask for its two-letter beginnings. The Spelling Bee Solver leaves the full spellings covered until you choose a reveal.

### Try the January 1 hive in the Spelling Bee Solver

The board above starts with A in the center and H, M, N, O, R and Y around it. These letters match the [January 1, 2024 puzzle](/spelling-bee-answers/2024-01-01). Its answer sheet contains 50 words worth 193 points, including one pangram. You can work through the example without opening the archive link, which leads to that dated puzzle.

#### Give yourself a stopping point

Before opening a group in the Spelling Bee Solver, decide what you want to learn. Say you have three four-letter words beginning with MA. The count tells you there is exactly one more on this dated board. You can return to the game with a small, specific problem to solve while keeping every spelling out of sight.

## How should I enter the seven hive letters?

Read the yellow center tile first and enter that letter at the start of the field. Then enter the six outer tiles in any order. You can type a single string such as AHMNORY, or paste the same letters with spaces or commas between them. Select **Find hints** once all seven tiles are present.

The Spelling Bee Solver treats the center as compulsory. An outer letter can appear several times in a word, once, or never. The order around the outside carries no meaning. If someone writes the example clockwise from Y and someone else starts from H, both can retrieve the same archived puzzle as long as A remains first.

### Fix a board with the wrong center

If all seven letters are correct but the required letter sits on the rim, tap that tile on the honeycomb. The Spelling Bee Solver moves it to the center and looks up the new board. Check the label beside the hive to see which letter every word now needs. You can tap the former center to move it back.

#### A repeated tile and a repeated letter are different

When copying the hive, each of its seven tiles should have a different letter. Inside a word, the same tile can be used repeatedly. NOON illustrates the distinction. Its two N letters and two O letters need only two tiles. On the sample A-centered board the spelling still fails, because neither tile supplies the required A.

## Where does the Spelling Bee Solver get its words?

When the center and all six outer letters match a completed puzzle in the WordleHint archive, you get the words listed for that date. The result identifies itself as an **Archived hive** and links to the puzzle. The displayed score is the sum of those entries, including their pangram bonuses.

For a **Custom hive**, the Spelling Bee Solver searches words that have appeared in completed archive puzzles. It keeps words of four or more letters that include your center and can be made from the seven tiles. The coverage date appears with the result. A word in this list has appeared somewhere in the archive. Its presence does not establish that a particular daily puzzle accepted it.

#### Why does one count change while the other stays at one?

Move H to the center in the Spelling Bee Solver. Words that contained A but lacked H can drop out. Words containing H can now qualify even if they omit A. A pangram already needs every tile, so changing which of those tiles is compulsory cannot by itself disqualify its letters. In this worked comparison, the two sources also agree on the pangram.

## How does the Spelling Bee Solver score different word lengths?

A four-letter word earns one point. Starting at five letters, a word earns its length in points. A pangram uses all seven different hive letters and adds seven bonus points. A seven-letter spelling that repeats a tile and misses a different one earns seven points. A seven-letter spelling that covers the whole hive earns fourteen.

Five-letter words contribute 95 of the 193 available points, almost half the board. The four-letter group is slightly larger, but finishing all twenty would earn just twenty points. If you need five points, finding one missing five-letter entry or five short entries would achieve the same thing. The Spelling Bee Solver gives you enough information to choose which search sounds more promising.

#### Check where the seven extra points went

The three seven-letter words would ordinarily add up to 21 points. One uses all seven tiles. Its bonus lifts the group total to 28. The eight-letter word on this board earns eight, so the longest entry is worth less than the pangram. You can confirm the values in the Spelling Bee Solver by opening each group and revealing its words after you finish playing.

## How do I read two-letter hints in the Spelling Bee Solver?

The Spelling Bee Solver can show the first two letters of entries within your selected group. Each pair comes with a count. On the example board, MA appears four times among the four-letter words, NA three times and RO three times. The rest of the prefixes make up the other ten. Switching to a different word length gives you a fresh set of counts.

### Count your found words against one prefix

Write your own four-letter words in alphabetical order and place a small mark beside each that begins with RO. If there are two, the Spelling Bee Solver tells you to look for one more. Keep R and O in place. There are only two positions left and one of them must carry A, so you can work on those two slots without scanning the whole honeycomb again.

#### Look for a familiar letter twice

Try saying the beginning out loud, then imagine the same vowel returning in the last position. The hive does not consume a letter when you use it. If a spelling needs an outside letter, discard it. For example, MARMOT uses the required A and several friendly-looking tiles, but the T rules it out of this particular hive.

### Reveal only what you meant to reveal

Choose **Show two-letter hints** if you want a starting pair. Choose **Reveal one word** if you want the first alphabetical spelling in that group and its score. **Reveal next word** works down the same group. **Reveal group** opens the entire selection. Switching groups closes the spellings again, so checking one length does not expose the next.

## Can I use the Spelling Bee Solver to find just the pangram?

Select the pangram group in the Spelling Bee Solver. You can leave it at a count, inspect its opening pairs, or reveal a spelling and its points. If you would like one more nudge before a full reveal, try a paper exercise. Write all seven letters in a row, cross them off as you build a word and look closely at the one that keeps getting left behind.

### Does a pangram have to be seven letters long?

Seven is the shortest possible length. A longer pangram can repeat one or more tiles and still use all seven. Count the distinct letters to decide whether it qualifies, then count every letter for its base score. An eight-letter pangram earns fifteen points. On a custom board the Spelling Bee Solver can also find zero pangrams, even when shorter candidates fit.

## Can the Spelling Bee Solver check a particular day?

Look for the date above the totals. If the same center and outer tiles match more than one completed puzzle, choose a date to read that puzzle’s own answer sheet. The Spelling Bee Solver keeps each sheet separate. If you already know the date but cannot remember the letters, it is faster to start from the [Spelling Bee answer archive](/spelling-bee-answers).

### What if the game rejected a candidate?

First check the center, the spelling and the source above your counts. A custom candidate may have been accepted in a different puzzle. If you have a specific word in mind, [Spelling Bee word history](/spelling-bee-word-history) lets you compare dates when its letters fit with dates when it was actually listed. That answers a different question from finding words for seven letters.

#### Which tool should I use while today’s game is open?

For clues prepared for the current puzzle, use [today’s Spelling Bee hints](/spelling-bee-hints-today). For a hand-entered board, the Spelling Bee Solver can still help you look for word families, repeated letters and possible pangrams. Read the source label before treating a total as the target for your game. You can always stop at the counts and finish the search yourself.
